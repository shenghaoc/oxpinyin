# Init timing gap: desktop timing and cloud attribution (2026-10-23 UTC)

Status: **attributed on one cloud host; gap not reproduced there.** Refs
#1090, #1106. Two campaigns are recorded. The desktop BDB campaign (10:37Z,
below) measured the warm gap: ox/pin init wall 1.005, 1.011, 1.049 and 1.040,
all intervals above 1.00. The cloud campaign (13:10Z–14:02Z) ran the new
init attribution stage on BDB and KC, subject, control and pin in one run on
one host. On that host the warm gap is not consistent: of eight init CPU
cells, six pass the control rule, and their ox/pin intervals are 1.008
[0.994, 1.032], 1.075 [1.041, 1.144], 0.960 [0.926, 0.994], 1.046
[1.023, 1.094], 1.010 [0.975, 1.041] and 1.020 [1.002, 1.101]. The
contributors below are the differences between ox and pin that hold in every
cell. They are named with
exact counts and sampled CPU shares, not with a timing ratio. No product code
changed. No captures were created, committed, attached or uploaded.

## Contributors

Exact counts are per init, warm, equal in every round and equal between ox
and control. "Share" is the fraction of init CPU samples (cpu-clock, 20 µs
period, 30 rounds per side; the 200 µs period gives the second figure). Pin
shares of `statx` and `unlink` are 0 because the pin makes no such call.

| # | contributor | exact count, ox vs pin | share of init CPU, ox | change that would remove it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | path and descriptor metadata calls | `statx` 34 (zhuyin), 37 (pinyin) vs 0, on BDB and KC | 1.1–2.2% / 0.8–1.8% | take type and size from one `fstat` of the opened descriptor; drop repeated path probes of the same file |
| 2 | failed sidecar unlinks | `unlink` 6 vs 0, all `ENOENT`, on BDB and KC | 0.4–0.9% / 0.3–1.1% | do not unlink `-lock`/`-shm` beside user DBM files on a backend that created none |
| 3 | extra mappings | `mmap` +7, `munmap` +3 to +6 | `mmap` +0.2–1.0 pp, `munmap` +0.0–0.6 pp over pin | one mapping per system chunk (#985); read the 27-byte user chunks instead of mapping them |
| 4 | minor page faults | +10 to +16 (BDB), +31 to +35 (KC) | page-fault share within ±3 pp of pin, either sign | spread over mappings (fault table): stack +3 to +4, user `.bin` maps +3, data files +3 to +5, facade +3 to +4 (BDB), heap +0 to +4 (BDB) and +15 (KC), libkyotocabinet +9 (KC) |
| 5 | cold major faults | 26/30 vs 19 (zhuyin/pinyin, BDB) | not timed | data files +4: none (the same pages; the pin reads them in `read()`); facade +4 (pinyin): row 6; libdb +4: not attributed further |

The pin answers the same reads with `open`, two `lseek`, two `read` and one
`mmap` per chunk (`src/include/memory_chunk.h:475-522` at 074a2219, read from
a clone fetched by that commit), and reads the small user chunks with `read`
(`memory_chunk.h:416-463`). It makes more `read` (+15 to +24) and `lseek`
(+21) calls, and on BDB more `close`, `getpid` and `newfstatat`; the trace
counts below give each.

Sources of contributors 1–3 in oxpinyin (by item):

- `statx`: three per system chunk file, two of them in
  `oxpinyin_data::phrase_library::map_chunk` (`Path::is_dir`,
  `File::metadata`); one per system DBM file in
  `oxpinyin_runtime::require_file` (`std::fs::metadata`); two for
  `table.conf` in `oxpinyin_runtime::read_conf_file` (`std::fs::metadata`,
  `std::fs::read`); one per `.dbin` and `user.conf` read through
  `std::fs::read` in `oxpinyin_user::persistence`; one per user DBM file from
  `Path::exists` in `load_bigram_rows`, `load_user_pinyin_index` and
  `load_user_phrase_index`; three per user `.bin` chunk.
- `unlink`: `oxpinyin_user::persistence::remove_dbm_sidecars`, called by
  `load_bigram_rows`, `load_user_pinyin_index` and `load_user_phrase_index`
  after each read: `user_bigram.db`, `user_phrase_index.bin` and
  `user_pinyin_index.bin`, each `-lock` and `-shm`.
- `mmap`: `phrase_library::map::MappedFile::from_file` and
  `MappedFile::private_copy` map each of `gb_char.bin`, `gbk_char.bin`,
  `merged.bin` and `opengram.bin` twice (the replay view, #985); `addon.bin`,
  `network.bin` and `user.bin` are mapped once each.

Not established on this host: the facade's own user time. Its share is above
the pin's in six cells and below in two (KC pinyin), while ox executes 2.3%
(BDB) and 2.4% (KC) fewer Ir in the facade itself (zhuyin phrases). libc and
DBM user time is lower for ox in seven of eight cells. Totals of the kernel
share differ from the pin by −0.4 to +4.2 pp, and control−ox spans −2.0 to
+3.2 pp, so the kernel total is not resolved either.

## Facade pages read by init

#1106 reported that cold init brings 118 (pinyin) and 106 (zhuyin) new pages
of the ox library into the page cache, against the pin's 71 and 52, and that
no other file differs. This campaign reproduces 118/106 for ox and measures
52 (pinyin) and 51 (zhuyin) for the pin; every other measured file agrees
within 4 pages. The page sets were identical in all 30 rounds of each side and
the same for both profiles. Pages are 4 KiB file pages of the measured object.

**Executed code.** Every facade function init executes in ox lies in the
ordered prefix; the only executed address outside it is the `.plt` stub. The
policy therefore covers init's code. It does not cluster it:

| facade | side | functions executed | distinct instructions | bytes of those functions | pages if contiguous | `.text` pages executed | of those, resident at M0 |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| zhuyin | ox | 125 | 14,101 | 239,203 | 59 | 74 (14–113) | 36 |
| zhuyin | pin | 47 | 2,873 | 18,168 | 5 | 19 (41–147) | 10 |
| pinyin | ox | 135 | 14,910 | 244,956 | 60 | 75 (16–119) | 29 |
| pinyin | pin | 57 | 3,237 | 20,002 | 5 | 20 (41–150) | 9 |

The policy's `init` tier (238 zhuyin, 254 pinyin paths) is the startup set of
load constructors, the init subtree and the exports to M1 for both profiles,
written in name order (`tools/generate-function-order.py`, `policy`). Init's
functions are interleaved with startup functions it does not run, so they
span 74/75 pages of a 104/106-page prefix. Their own bodies need 59/60 pages.
The largest by distinct instructions executed, zhuyin (pinyin's ten are the
same functions, with `AddonDictionary::open_with` for `without_indexes` and
`pinyin_init` for `zhuyin_init`):
`oxpinyin_runtime::Runtime::open_with_law` (pages 39–44, 2,346),
`GenericUserStore<BdbStore>::open_session` (62–69, 891),
`persistence::load_logs_with_mask` (89–94 and 96, 794),
`store_libpinyin::seed_txn_with_replay` (101, 103–106, 469),
`persistence::load_checked_impl` (84–86, 447),
`persistence::check_format` (83–84, 438),
`AddonDictionary::without_indexes` (28–29, 380), `zhuyin_init` (111–113,
333), `persistence::system_originals` (97–99, 313),
`table_conf::Lambda::from_decimal` (29–30, 309). The pin's largest are
`zhuyin_init` (306), `SystemTableInfo2::load` (265) and
`SubPhraseIndex::merge` (261).

**Touched data.** ox zhuyin touches 7 `.rodata` pages in init, all read
during init: 447, 449, 474 and 482 (no sized symbol), 452
(`core::unicode::unicode_data::grapheme_extend::OFFSETS`), 456
(`core::num::dec2flt::table::POWER_OF_FIVE_128`) and 480
(`oxpinyin_core::string_tables::SECONDARY_ZHUYIN_INDEX`). pinyin touches 9
(498, 500, 501, 503, 505 `grapheme_extend`, 508 `POWER_OF_FIVE_128`, 526,
532 `SECONDARY_ZHUYIN_INDEX`, 534). The touched `.data.rel.ro` pages (3) and
`.data` page (`OPEN_STORES`) were already resident at M0. The pin touches 4
(zhuyin) and 6 (pinyin) `.rodata` pages, 3 and 5 of them read during init,
among them `valid_zhuyin_table`. The ordering policy does not cover `.rodata`,
`.data.rel.ro` or `.eh_frame`.

**Why ox reads more pages.** (a) +29 (zhuyin) / +35 (pinyin) executed `.text`
pages: init runs 12–13 times as many bytes of facade functions, spread across
the name-ordered prefix. (b) The second run of pages read, around the touched
`.rodata`, is 63/64 pages against the pin's 18/19: the last 14 `.text` pages
after the prefix, the whole 37-page `.rodata` and 13 pages of
`.eh_frame_hdr`/`.eh_frame` that init never touches. Changes that would
remove them: order the `init` tier by first use, or place init's own
functions before the other startup functions (the floor is 59/60 pages);
shrink the code init runs; place the `.rodata` objects init touches together
or stop touching them in init (the Unicode and float-parsing tables come
from the standard library). None is implemented here.
